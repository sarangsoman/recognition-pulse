Recognition Desk

An assistant for managers who mean to recognise their team and don't get round to it. Plain rules find who is due. AI drafts the words. You edit and send.

How it decides who is due
  • Work anniversary within 21 days (1 year or more): highest priority.
  • Peers recognised them, you haven't: 2 or more peer recognitions in 90 days and none from you.
  • Silent: no recognition from anyone for 30+ days (45+ is marked urgent).
  • Birthday within 7 days: a light-touch note.
  • The AI sees only first name, level, the reason, years at company, the value they showed and your optional note. It is told not to invent facts. Claude drafting is off until you tick \u201cUse Claude to draft\u201d (it uses a small amount of hosting credit per draft). Otherwise, or if it is unavailable, a template is used.

All people, teams and dates are synthetic (generated by a script, as of 15 Nov 2026). Nothing is stored and "Send" does not message anyone. Part of Sarang Soman's product portfolio; the same data powers the Recognition Pulse dashboard.
